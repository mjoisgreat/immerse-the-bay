Living Window

A campfire story you can step into.

One person speaks, the room becomes the story, and everyone watching shapes what happens next, live.

In the headset, use your hands: pinch and draw a square to open a window, or ask Bonnie out loud: "Bonnie, open a window" · raise one to your mouth to talk (Bonnie comes to listen) · part both like curtains to step inside · shake both to close a window · pinch a window to move it, with both hands to resize it (A/X, B/Y and grip do the same)
On your phone: hold 🎙 to talk · double-tap a window to step inside · press and hold empty space, then draw a rectangle, to open a window · double-tap empty space to call Bonnie · ⋯ More → Touch gestures for the rest
On desktop: click empty space to open a window · drag to look · hold Space to talk · double-click a window to step inside · H shows or hides the buttons

Touch gestures

  • 🎙Hold the mic and speak: tell the story, or talk to Bonnie
  • 👆Tap a window to pick it · tap it again to touch the world there
  • ✌️Double-tap a window, or spread two fingers, to step inside · double-tap or pinch in to step out
  • ✍️Press and hold empty space, then draw a rectangle, to open a window there
  • ❤️Press and hold empty space, then draw a heart: Bonnie answers with hearts
  • ✊Press and hold a window, then drag, to move it
  • 🤏Pinch a window with two fingers to resize it
  • ☝️Flick a window up twice to close it
  • 🐰Double-tap empty space to call Bonnie · tap her to follow or stay · drag to carry, let go to throw
  • 📱With the camera on, move your phone to look around · otherwise drag
  • 👐Two-finger tap hides or shows the buttons
