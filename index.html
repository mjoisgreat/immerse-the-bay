Living Window

A campfire story you can step into.

One person speaks, the room becomes the story, and everyone watching shapes what happens next, live.

In the headset, use your hands: raise one to your mouth to talk · part both like curtains to step inside · shake both to close a window (A/X, B/Y and grip do the same)
On desktop: click empty space to open a window · drag to look · hold Space to talk · double-click a window to step inside · H shows or hides the buttons
Starts a fresh story in this world and opens its first window now, so it is already live when the headset goes on (a window takes 20–30 s to reach video).
